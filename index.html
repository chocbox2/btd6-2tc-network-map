This visualization represents the "Two Towers CHIMPS" challenge in the game, the challenge where you must finish the gamemode "CHIMPS" with exactly two towers.
In the game, the towers are sorted into categories based on their traits, represented by the different colors of nodes.
Each link between towers represents a tower combination that synergizes well enough to win the challenge.
Think of this like a "social media network" of towers, where the links represent a connection between towers.

2 Towers CHIMPS Network Map

Hover over a tower node to see the name of the tower and # of connections the tower has.
Larger nodes have more connections
Click on a tower to highlight it and any connected towers. Click again to deselect.
Drag the tower along the map to change the layout of the network. On release, forces will pull the tower back into equilibrium, based on it's connected towers
